# TradeVault

**A private trading journal for futures traders: log every trade, review your days, and see what's really working, on your Windows PC and your iPhone.**

TradeVault keeps your whole trading life in one place: trades, premarket plans, daily and weekly reviews, rules, your playbook, chart screenshots, goals and a life journal. It turns them into the numbers and reports that show where your edge is and where it leaks. Everything stays on your own devices. There's no account and no subscription, and nothing you log is ever uploaded.

- **Windows app:** [download the latest version](https://github.com/motherofstacy/TradeVault/releases/latest) (`TradeVault_…_x64-setup.exe`)
- **iPhone:** open [motherofstacy.github.io/TradeVault](https://motherofstacy.github.io/TradeVault/) in Safari, then tap Share, then Add to Home Screen
- **Moving data between them:** see [Phone and PC: backups, merging and updates](Phone-and-PC.md)

## What it does

### Log trades your way
- Add trades by hand, with setup, stop, target, mistakes, market conditions, notes and screenshots
- Import fills and trade lists from **TradingView, NinjaTrader, Tradovate and Rithmic**, or a CSV, by dragging the file in
- Commissions and fees come from **your own per-contract rates**
- A position size calculator, and tilt warnings after a run of losses

### See where your edge is
- A **dashboard** with net P&L, win rate, profit factor, expectancy, average R, average win and loss, and max drawdown
- Equity curves, daily P&L, rolling stats, and breakdowns by setup, side, time of day, session, hold time, mistakes, mood and sleep
- A **calendar** of your trading days, plans, reviews and life entries
- A **weekly report card** with a grade, plus monthly and yearly reports

### Run your accounts like a business
- Multiple accounts and account groups, with a leader and followers for copy trading
- **Prop firm tracking:** evaluation and funded accounts, drawdown limits (fixed or trailing), profit targets, minimum days, consistency rules, payouts and certificates
- **Odds of passing:** a simulation of your chances of passing an evaluation at different sizes

### Build the habits that make a trader
- **Premarket plan:** bias, key levels, news and a checklist before the open
- **Daily and weekly reviews** with your own questions and choice boxes
- **Rules** and a **playbook** of your setups
- **Life journal:** mood, energy, sleep and habits, with trends and a year-in-pixels view, so you can see how life outside the screen shows up in your trading
- **Goals** with progress, milestones and due dates
- Reminders on the dashboard, and in the Windows app as Windows notifications

### Make it yours
- Rename, reorder or hide any tab, and add your own text and choice boxes anywhere
- Backgrounds (Blueprint, Chart paper with an optional moving chart, Dot grid, Topographic, Carbon fiber, your own color or picture), dark and light themes
- Keyboard shortcuts you can change, plus your own (Back up now is Ctrl+B)
- An optional PIN lock screen

## The Windows app

- **Saves as you go:** your journal saves to a file on your PC as you work
- **Backs itself up:** a backup each time it opens, every 15 minutes while it's open, and before anything risky, to a folder you choose. Your own backups are never deleted
- **Pictures as files:** screenshots are saved as ordinary image files next to your journal
- **Lives in the tray**, can open when you sign in to Windows, and remembers its size and place
- **Updates itself:** when a new version is out, TradeVault offers **Update now**, backs up your journal first, and restarts on the new version. Every update is signed, and TradeVault refuses anything that wasn't

The first time you run the installer, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't from a big publisher. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## The iPhone version

The same journal, running as a home-screen app in Safari. Log trades and reviews during the day, then bring them into your PC with **Merge into my journal**. Saving a backup opens the iPhone share sheet, so you can mail it to yourself or save it to Files. After you open it once with a connection, it also opens with no internet at all, and it picks up new versions the next time you're online.

## Your privacy

- Your journal lives **only on your devices**: in the Windows app's folder on your PC, and in Safari's storage on your iPhone.
- This GitHub page only hosts the empty app. It never receives your trades, notes or pictures.
- Moving data between devices is always something you do yourself, with a backup file you control.
- **Never upload a backup file to GitHub.** This repository is public.
